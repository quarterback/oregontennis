## Question 2: Individual Daily Match Limits (USTA Rules)

### USTA/OSAA Match Limitations

Per [USTA Junior Tournament Regulations](https://www.usta.com/content/dam/usta/2025-pdfs/ntl-jr-regulations-2025.pdf):

| Match Type | Daily Limit | Notes |
|------------|-------------|-------|
| 3-set matches | 2 per day | Standard for championship play |
| 2-set matches (pro-set) | 3 per day | Used in some regular season |
| Combined (main + consolation) | 3 per day max | 2 singles + 1 doubles OR 1 singles + 2 doubles |

### Application to Team + Individual Tournaments

**Scenario:** Player competing in BOTH team playoffs AND individual state tournament

**Key Issue:** If team semifinals (May 8) overlap with individual tournament dates (May 22-24), there is NO conflict.

**If same-day competition occurred:**
- Player in team match: 1 team match counts as 1 match
- Player in individual singles: Counts as separate match
- Player in individual doubles: Counts as separate match
- **Maximum same day:** 2 three-set matches OR 3 two-set matches

**OSAA Individual Tournament Standard:**
- State Individual Tournament: May 22-24, 2025
- Format: Best 2 of 3 sets
- Daily limit applies across all competition

## Question 4: Court Requirement Change (4 → 2)

### Original Recommendation: 4 Courts Minimum

**Rationale:**
- Allows all 8 singles + 4 doubles to play simultaneously
- Faster match completion (~2.5 hours)
- Preferred for large programs

### Revised Recommendation: 2 Courts Minimum

**Rationale:**

1. **Roster Reality:** 247 of 251 teams (98.4%) have 6+ players - enough to field a complete lineup

2. **Match Flow with 2 Courts:**
   | Wave | Court 1 | Court 2 | Time |
   |------|---------|---------|------|
   | 1 | Singles #1 | Singles #2 | 0:00-1:00 |
   | 2 | Singles #3 | Singles #4 | 1:00-2:00 |
   | 3 | Doubles #1 | Doubles #2 | 2:00-3:00 |

   **Total time: ~3 hours** (vs ~2.5 hours with 4 courts)

3. **Accessibility:** Many schools, especially rural and small-classification, cannot access 4-court facilities

4. **Precedent:** Most first-round playoff hosting requires only available courts, not ideal facilities

5. **Flexibility:** Higher seeds with 4+ courts can still use them; 2-court minimum ensures ALL qualifying teams can host

### Key Point:
The 2-court minimum is a **floor**, not a ceiling. Teams with more courts should use them. This change ensures no team is excluded from hosting due to facility limitations.

## Appendix: Travel Distance Data (from Baseball/Softball Analysis)

### Relevance to Tennis

Tennis combines classifications (4A/3A/2A/1A) creating WIDER geographic spread than single-class baseball/softball.

From our 11-year baseball/softball analysis:
- 63% of playoff games require 95+ mile travel
- Regional re-pairing could reduce travel by **39%**
- Seeds 13-16 reach quarterfinals only **3%** of the time

**Tennis Application:**
- Regional first-round pods for lower seeds = major travel reduction
- Minimal competitive impact (bottom half rarely advances anyway)
- Multi-day tournament format makes travel burden WORSE than single-game format
